# Canal

Textos y recursos fijos del canal **Lamplight Sessions** (@Lamplight-sessions). Los archivos de imagen viven en `media/marca/` (fuera de git).

## Descripción del canal

```text
Find your focus under a warm lamp.

Lamplight Sessions creates long, calm study videos: a quiet window onto a rainy city, a snowy cabin or a misty harbour at night, softly looping while mellow lofi music plays. Put one on in the background to study, read, work, write or simply unwind.

Every Session pairs a new place with an original soundtrack of lofi, jazz and ambient piano, with gentle rain or city sounds woven in. No sudden changes, no distractions: just a steady atmosphere for deep work.

🕯 A new Session every week
🎧 Lofi · chillhop · jazz · ambient piano
🌧 Rain, night cities, cabins, harbours and more

Visuals and music are created with AI and carefully curated by Lamplight Sessions.

Subscribe and keep the lamp on.
```
